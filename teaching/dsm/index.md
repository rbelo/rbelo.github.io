Interactive simulations used in the MSc course *Digital Strategy and Markets* at Nova SBE. Each one runs in the browser, with no installation.

- [The production function](production-function.html) — a service desk chooses how much to spend on AI tools. One slider changes the price of the tools and the desk slides along its curve; the other improves organization and the whole curve lifts.
- [Substitutes and complements](substitution-and-complementarity.html) — can one input replace the other? Bend the curve from inputs that must be used together to inputs that stand in for each other, and watch how the cheapest mix responds to a price change.
