---
title: "Improving job seekers’ odds with Large Language Models: randomized controlled trials at Instituto de Emprego e Formação Profissional"
---

<img src="/assets/images/iefp_logo.png" alt="IEFP Logo" style="float: right; margin-left: 20px; max-width: 350px;"/>

This project is a collaboration with the [Instituto de Emprego e Formação Profissional](https://www.iefp.pt/) (IEFP) to improve job seekers' outcomes by using Large Language Models (LLMs) to generate personalized job search advice. The project involves running randomized controlled trials to assess the effectiveness of LLM-generated advice compared to traditional methods.
